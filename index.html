Powder Hound

Loading snow conditions across Europe

Figures come from weather models (mostly DWD ICON and ECMWF, picked automatically per region), sampled at one reference elevation per region. They show where snow is likely and how regions compare. They are not station measurements and say nothing about a specific slope.

Avalanche danger is not part of this data. Before any off-piste or touring day, read the official bulletin linked in each card.
